Entries are drawn from published government contract notices, National Audit Office reports, Public Accounts Committee findings and local news reporting, with a link to the original source on every entry. The absurdity score is editorial judgement, not an official measure — it weighs budget overruns, lack of public justification, and whether the spend demonstrably helped the people paying for it. The total above sums the headline figure quoted in each source; some are contract ceilings or estimates rather than confirmed final costs.
